How It Works

Simple from start to finish

AnyTax is designed to take the complexity out of self-assessment and business tax. Here's how it works.

1

Connect

Link your bank accounts and income sources. AnyTax securely imports your financial data so everything is in one place.

2

Categorise

Smart categorisation automatically sorts your income and expenses. Review and adjust with a few clicks.

3

Calculate

Real-time tax calculations show exactly what you owe, with potential savings highlighted for your review.

4

Submit

File your return directly to HMRC from within AnyTax. Tracked, timestamped, and fully compliant.
